Creates an OTLP/HTTP exporter that POSTs to endpoint.

headers are merged into every request on top of the default Content-Type: application/json header. serviceName is reported as the service.name resource attribute. An optional httpClient may be injected for testing; otherwise the exporter owns and closes its client in close.
